Privacy and GDPR

Privacy notice

This notice explains how Decisionary handles personal data submitted through the landing page and pilot request form.

Who controls the data

The controller is Decisionary. For privacy questions, contact us at decisionary.app@gmail.com.

What we collect

When you send a pilot or demo request, we collect the information you provide in the form, such as name, work email, and scenario details.

Why we use it

We use this data to respond to your enquiry, prepare relevant pilot follow-up, and keep a basic business record of the conversation. We do not sell this data.

Legal basis

We process enquiry data based on your request before entering into a potential business relationship and, where relevant, our legitimate interest in responding to product enquiries.

Retention

We keep enquiry data only as long as needed to handle the request and reasonable follow-up, unless a longer period is required for legal or business records.

Your rights

Depending on your location, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, or data portability. You may also contact your local data protection authority.

Cookies

This static landing page does not intentionally set analytics or marketing cookies. If that changes, we will update this notice and add the required consent controls.
