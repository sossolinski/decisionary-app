The gap

Airline crises do not test discussion. They test decision flow.

Without Decisionary

The exercise happens, then the trail disappears.

  • Ops updates, media signals, and legal notes live in different tools.
  • Holding-line rationale is reconstructed after the fact.
  • Owners and deadlines depend on manual follow-up after the room ends.
With Decisionary

Every signal can become a decision, task, or traceable outcome.

  • OCC, communications, legal, and leadership work from one live feed.
  • Escalations create ownership while the exercise is still running.
  • Debrief evidence is captured as the aviation scenario unfolds.
